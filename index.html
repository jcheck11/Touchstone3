Our Mission

Here at Twin Cities Animal Rescue we rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome both dogs and cats across the Minneapolis area. We beleive all animals deserve safety, care, a loving family, and a place to call home.

Our Impact

  • Over 1,000 animals adopted since 2018
  • 100+ volunteers donating their time and care every year
  • 50+ active foster homes
  • 2,000+ meals served in our shelter's every month
